existing byte/entry ceilings. No row-country or field-eligibility decision is
cached across occurrences. Parser certificates are computed once from the
complete ordered component stream into a private derived index. Giant accepted
component scalars still stream individually. Component/member caches retain only
complete small sequences, preserving Q5 forward/reverse alignment and evidence.

The complete context join is materialized once into a private, externally sorted
work index, with exact key totality checked against the accepted-derived context
keys. This is neither an output relation nor a replacement source authority.
The operational SQL wrapper shares an input-revision counter across cursors;
explicit mutations of private join inputs invalidate/rebuild this derivative
index. Existing source-slot/country corruption tests therefore still fail safely.
Subsequent binary-range pages scan that sorted/indexed table, avoiding repeated
upstream joins at every page. Its disk cost is measured during confirmation.
Context input pages close their cursor before generation. Diagnostic INSERTs are
columnar batches of at most 64 contexts and an estimated 8 MiB, with one oversized
accepted scalar permitted without truncation. Database checkpoints verify no WAL
remains after the batch. WAL observations are sampled before/after checkpoints,
not continuous maxima; initialization/recovery WAL is reported separately from
generation. The operational bound is one diagnostic batch plus engine overhead
and a possible oversized scalar, not a fixed scientific address-size limit.

Original 16 hexadecimal prefixes remain canonical output partitions. Each has
independently validated/resumable units of at most 64 contexts. The unit identity
binds exact input/context/science/executable identities and exact ordered context
keys. Completed units are copied and revalidated into a new attempt; incomplete

index and prefix record reconcile all units. A streaming coalesce creates normal
bounded-size final relation files. Unit boundaries/path names/telemetry do not
